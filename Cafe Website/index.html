About Us

At My Cafe, we are passionate about serving freshly prepared food made with quality ingredients. Our goal is to provide delicious meals, excellent coffee, and friendly service in a cozy environment where everyone feels at home.

Today we continue using the same oven to prepare fresh food. We use local vegetables, fresh ingredients, and coffee from nearby roasters.
